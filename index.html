Each project is explained end to end on its own page: the problem, what I did, the tools, who I worked with, the result and the limits.
ProjectWhat happenedWhat it does not show
Zest pillar page rebuildZest, 2026. SEO and conversion Search position rose from around 12 to 2. The page produced two demo requests in its first 30 days. Two demo requests is a small sample, and several things changed at once. I did not run a controlled test.
Semrush newsletter turnaroundSemrush, Oct 2024 to Oct 2025 Took a dead list and built it back up to about 6,000 engaged readers within a few months. Paid acquisition was part of the rebuild, so the growth was not organic.
Zest ROI calculatorZest, 2026. Search content and a tool A calculator, and the article around it, for budgeting a social climate survey. Zest's in-house experts validated the formula, and Yapluqua picked it up. Press pickup shows the calculator was noticed. It is not a measure of leads.
Zest audience and messaging workZest, 2023 Buyer personas, pain points and more than 100 hooks, built from competitor and customer review research and tested in a cold email campaign. No metric for this work.
Go-to-market strategy for a venture advisoryPrivate client, 2026 A diagnosis of broken unit economics, a five-step roadmap and a live financial model covering 18 configurations. These are model projections built on assumed starting figures.
