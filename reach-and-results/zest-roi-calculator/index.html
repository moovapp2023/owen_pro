Zest ROI calculator

A calculator, and the article around it, for working out the return on a social climate survey before budgeting for one. The formula was validated by Zest's in-house experts. It was picked up by a respected outlet in the space (Yapluqua).

Client
Zest, French HR software
Year
2026
Type of work
Search content and a tool
My role
Researched, built and wrote it

The problem

A long-tail search question: how to allocate a budget for a social climate survey. I reasoned that the best answer was to establish the return on the survey first, and then talk about the budget.

What I did

  1. Gathered industry research to ground the calculation.
  2. Built the formula for the return on social climate measurement. Zest's in-house experts validated it.
  3. Turned the formula into an interactive calculator.
  4. Wrote the article around it. I researched, wrote and finalised the whole article myself.

Tools, and how they fit together

The calculator is a web tool I built in HTML, CSS and JavaScript, sitting inside the article on Zest's site.

Who I worked with

Zest's in-house experts checked the formula. The article is published under a colleague's byline, but I am its author.

Result

The calculator was picked up by a respected outlet in the space, Yapluqua,.
