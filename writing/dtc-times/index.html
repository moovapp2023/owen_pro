DTC Times

The DTC Times is a newsletter covering direct-to-consumer marketing, ecommerce strategy and brand building for practitioners. Below are the issues I would point you to, with the best section of each named.

Issues are bylined The DTC Times. I wrote them (Mar to Aug 2024), with an editor fact-checking. I sourced and wrote the stories each week, and the editor fact-checked them and either green-lit or rejected each one.

Yay deepfakes (boo, actually)

TikTok is enabling the use of deepfakes. Brands will be able to buy ads featuring AI imitations of generic people, OR OF SPECIFIC CREATORS. It’s happening, folks.

They claim it’s to accelerate content production and help brands access different language bases more easily. Which deepfakes totally do, no debate there, but once the floodgates open, they will be hard to close.

Issue 4, Headwinds and Tailwinds

Read this partHow the pandemic changed everything for outdoor DTC, with 7.1 million new participants in 2020 and the brands that became giants overnight.

What goes up…

Unprecedented demand, plus a generous investment era (ZIRP) encouraged aggressive expansion by these booming companies. They pulled 5+ years of demand forward over the course of about 24 months, demolishing expectations and drastically skewing inventory projections.

Like a snake trying to swallow an elephant, the big bump in inventory got stuck due to supply chain disruptions.

New Amazon Fees

Amazon recently introduced new fees for seller inventory management in its warehouses.

What does this mean in practice? It gets complicated. Inbound placement fees charge you for sending all of your inventory to one warehouse. Split your package between 4+ warehouses and the fee goes away. But now you’re paying more for shipping.
